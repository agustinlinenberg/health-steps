Privacy policy

Last updated September 9, 2026.

Health steps is a personal app for Agustin Linenberg. It reads daily activity step counts from his own Oura account so a daily step target can be tracked. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or give medical advice.

Data read: daily activity steps only. No other Oura data is requested.

That count is used only to show steps so far, how many remain to the current target, and whether a short walk would close the gap. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

The Oura connection can be removed from the Oura account settings. Questions: agustin.linenberg@gmail.com.
